# LeadFlow Workspace — Design Brainstorm

## Three Directions Considered

### 1. Brass & Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial operations desk inspired by field notebooks, stamped paperwork, and focused independent work. It makes sales execution feel deliberate rather than salesy.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Coastal Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A luminous, breezy coordination interface built around maritime navigation cues and calm blue-space rhythm. It makes small-business outreach feel approachable and progressive.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. After-Hours Control Room
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark operational console with restrained signal colors and concentrated information density. It makes the project workflow feel like an active command center.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Brass & Ledger

### Design Movement
Editorial functionalism mixed with modern stationery design. The interface should feel like a refined working folio: deliberate typography, tactile paper surfaces, and calm high-contrast information blocks.

### Core Principles
1. **Work in the open:** The current priority, its owner, and the next action are always visible without a hunt.
2. **Calm command:** Warm space and ink-dark framing reduce stress while signal red focuses the eye only where decisions are needed.
3. **Editorial hierarchy:** Dense operational information is shaped with headlines, marginal notes, stamps, and ruled structure rather than generic dashboard cards.
4. **One project, one path:** The product follows a prospect from first observation through delivery and follow-up as a clear, contiguous journey.

### Color Philosophy
Parchment and mineral-grey surfaces communicate approachability and craft. Ink navy carries structure and trust. A restrained vermilion is reserved for forward motion—unread tasks, deadlines, and the primary action—rather than used as decoration. Moss green indicates complete, verified work.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a fixed, notebook-like left rail for the lifecycle, a broad asymmetric work canvas, and a narrow “next move” edge panel on larger screens. The home dashboard behaves like an active briefing sheet, not a card grid. On smaller screens, the rail becomes a horizontal journey strip.

### Signature Elements
1. A bold stacked "LF" monogram that feels stamped rather than digitally drawn.
2. Fine ledger rules, marginal labels, and section index numerals.
3. Small vermilion “action tabs” that anchor active tasks and CTA controls.

### Interaction Philosophy
Actions should make state visibly change: completing a delivery task advances the project progress, choosing a template opens it into the working canvas, and context-aware buttons confirm what will happen. Use straightforward local interactions and avoid dead-end menu items.

### Animation
Use a brief 180–240ms custom ease-out for hovering, opening panels, and changing active navigation. Let task completion use a compact checkmark transition and a one-time toast. Keep motion focused on opacity and transform; respect reduced-motion settings.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for major headings and occasional emphasis, paired with **Manrope** for interface text, lists, and forms. Headings should be large and editorial; labels should be compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced. Never use Inter.

### Brand Essence
**A complete field desk for independent operators who want to sell and deliver a focused service with confidence.**

Personality: **grounded, precise, encouraging.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, decisive, and action-led. CTAs are specific about the next move. Microcopy should make operating pressure feel manageable, not inflated.

Example lines:
- “Turn a warm observation into a booked kickoff.”
- “One lead source. One clear workflow. Seven days.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact, stacked **LF** letterform inside a vermilion stamp shape with a small offset notch, paired with a simple "LeadFlow" wordmark in Manrope. The mark should be readable at header scale and reused as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Ledger Vermilion — #C44832**
